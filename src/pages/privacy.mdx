# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-12_

TrueNAS Pulse does not collect personal data through analytics, tracking,
advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

<PrivacyLabel hideLink />


## What the app does

TrueNAS Pulse connects **directly to your configured servers**. It communicates with the TrueNAS
servers that you configure and contains no in-app analytics, tracking,
advertising, crash-reporting, or generic telemetry SDKs.

API tokens are used to authenticate requests directly to those servers. Alert
notifications are generated locally on your device; the app has no remote push
delivery service. Background refresh is best effort and subject to iOS scheduling,
so timely alerts are not guaranteed while the app is backgrounded or closed.

## Data not collected

- No analytics or tracking SDKs
- No crash reporting services
- No advertising identifiers
- No data shared with third parties
- No remote alert relay traffic

## Where your data lives

- **API tokens** are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
- **Server metadata** (URLs, names, ordering) is stored locally using
  `UserDefaults` on your device.
- **Local diagnostics**, when present, stay on the device and are never
  uploaded.
- If you enable **iCloud Sync** (Pro), your monitor configurations and server
  list sync across your devices via Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store. API
  tokens remain stored in the Keychain and are excluded from iCloud Sync.
  They are used only to authenticate directly to your configured servers.
- **Alert and monitor notification data** stays on the device. iOS controls when
  background refresh can run; the app does not continuously monitor servers
  while closed.

## App Store privacy details

For App Store Connect's "Data Not Collected" disclosure: TrueNAS Pulse
collects no data from users, links no data to identity, and does no tracking.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the change will be summarised here with a new
"last updated" date. The app itself does not have the means to notify you of
a change because there is no telemetry channel — please check this page when
updating to a new version if you want to review what (if anything) changed.
